Privacy policy

How this site handles cookies, advertising, analytics, email, and the limited data needed to run a public-record publication.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Procurement Archaeology is an independent research publication edited by Abdulrehman Bhatti. The site is published at procurementarchaeology.com. For privacy questions, email bhattiabdulrehman430@gmail.com.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your theme preference and your cookie choice. These values are stored locally in your browser and are not used to identify you across other websites. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

The site also loads Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and limit how often you see an ad. Google's own privacy policy explains how it uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Advertising supports the research. Advertisers do not receive editorial approval, advance copies, or the right to change a finding. The site does not sell a favourable article. If a sponsorship or commercial relationship is ever added, it will be disclosed on the relevant page.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and opt-out controls at Google's advertising page and Google Ads Settings.

Analytics and server logs

The site may receive standard server logs from its hosting provider, including IP address, browser type, requested page, and timestamp. These logs are used for security, debugging, and aggregate traffic measurement. They are not used to build a personal profile.

Email

If you email a correction, tip, or enquiry, the message and your email address are used to respond and, where appropriate, to check the record. Correspondence may be kept for editorial accountability. Do not send personal data that is not necessary for the enquiry.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser. You can use the cookie banner on the home page to accept or decline the site's local storage choice. You can contact the publication to ask about correspondence you have sent.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's tools or advertising arrangements change. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
